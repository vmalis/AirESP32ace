Airport city reference data

Source: OurAirports, https://ourairports.com/data/
Data dictionary: https://ourairports.com/help/data-dictionary.html
Downloaded November 18, 2026. Public domain; no accuracy warranty.

AirportCities.json is a reduced offline fallback, not a live status source.
It covers unambiguous IATA and ICAO codes for open airports with a known
primary municipality. GPS/local identifiers and closed airports are excluded.
Parenthetical locality qualifiers are omitted for compact display labels.
Provider city names take precedence. Unknown codes, country mismatches, and
locations more than 100 km from the reference are not guessed.

The source SHA-256 is embedded in the JSON. Regenerate with
scripts/generate_airport_city_catalog.py and a fresh OurAirports airports.csv.
This dataset is shipped only with the Mac app. Resolved labels are included
in each prescribed flight and persist on the device; no extra API calls,
firmware-linked database, or changes to the eight device cities are needed.
